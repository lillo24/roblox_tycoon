# Infinite property rules

`Catalogue` owns stable Infinite identities, prices, footprints and appearances. Session's four-upgrade catalogue is separate. `Placement` owns grid and footprint validation shared by local preview and authoritative transactions.

Style: warm metal, clay and garden greens. Reserve 8×8 for presses/workshops, 10×10 for later greenhouses, 12×10 for observatories and 4×4 for plants. The six authored lots support a local X envelope of −25..25 and Z −19..28. Initially Z ends at 12; a later rear-garden expansion opens 12..28 without moving existing objects. The fixed starter occupies the front apron; the ten-stud central route and two-stud object gaps remain clear. One-stud coordinates and quarter turns keep footprint checks exact. Stored equipment keeps earning; all income remains online only.

INF-03 adds 22 finite discoveries: 13 unique earning machines (including the four inherited units), one fixed expansion and eight repeatable decoration types. Ceramics, Garden and Sky each have 12/90/450 cash stages earning +2/+5/+8 per second. All directions can coexist. Rear garden costs 220 and opens the reserved 800 square studs; existing transforms never move.

Add content by giving Catalogue a stable ID, bounded footprint, trusted price/rate and acyclic prerequisite; add its silhouette/activity in Models. Keep retired IDs in ById with `retired = true` and retain their Models/income rules: hide new sales, preserve owned copies. Do not reuse an ID for a different footprint or remove prerequisites of owned items without a migration. Unknown IDs still refuse rather than lose possessions. Data v1/v2 upgrades to v3; store, account keys and receipt envelope stay unchanged.

Limits: 64 total belongings, of which at most 48 new decorations. The reservation leaves room for all 14 unique progression items even for a migrated v2 account full of decorations. At most 100 income per definition, 1,000,000 cost, and 12×10 footprint; validator checks prerequisites/cycles. Owned equipment earns in storage. Cash retains its exact-integer ceiling. Client activity runs at 10 Hz only for visible objects within 120 studs and respects reduced motion; no simulated products, server animation traffic or saved debris. PropertyWorld reconciles accepted revisions, retaining unchanged objects and ground. Models.create marks the parts that use the palette; Models.setTheme updates those colors and animated RestColor in place. Move/re-place rebuilds the affected silhouette with new RestFrame/OrbitCenter metadata. Cash ticks do not rebuild geometry.
